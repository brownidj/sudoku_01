Invitation: 20 testers wanted for a closed test of SuDoKu Fresh

I’m looking for up to 20 players to help with a closed test of SuDoKu Fresh, a seniors-friendly Sudoku-style app designed to be colourful, relaxed, and enjoyable.

The app is especially intended for players who enjoy Sudoku but prefer a friendlier, less intimidating experience, with clear visuals and a relaxed feel.
